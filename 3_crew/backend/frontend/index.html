Architecture

How the handoff works

Three agents gather evidence in parallel. manager_agent owns the handoff: it reconciles what they return, flags disagreement instead of silently picking a side, and emits one structured recommendation.

Example handoff: On TOYT, financial_researcher's fundamentals read constructive while financial_analyst and stock_picker lean sell-leaning — manager_agent flags that conflict instead of averaging it away, which is why the stance shows Hold with a visible "conflicting signals" note. On Shield AI, manager_agent overrides stock_picker's own pick, returning Insufficient Data once financial_researcher and financial_analyst can't verify the underlying financials — Shield AI is privately held, so there's no public filing to check.
